Bastion Server Control Panel

Features

One Windows control panel and Discord bot for running your server. Everything below is included in the Standard plan.

  • Control panel
  • Tickets
  • Moderation
  • Announcements
  • Onboarding
  • Server management
  • Safety & records
  • Account & updates

Control panel

  • Setup AssistantConnect your bot step by step, with permission and role checks before anything is saved.
  • DashboardBot status, connected server, open tickets, and live CPU and RAM use.
  • Tray modeRuns quietly in the system tray, can start with Windows, and alerts you to new tickets and reports.

Support tickets

  • Ticket panel/setup-tickets posts a button panel. Members open private ticket channels.
  • Staff toolsClaim, close, reopen, add a member, and delete with confirmation, from Discord or the control panel.
  • TranscriptsClosed tickets are saved as transcripts in your private ticket log channel.

Moderation

  • Member reports/report lets members privately flag someone to your staff.
  • ActionsWarn, time out, remove a time-out, kick and ban from the member directory, with a reason on every action.
  • Word filterRemoves messages with blocked words, catches look-alike spellings, and logs the action without keeping the message.
  • Purge/purge bulk-deletes recent messages after confirmation.
  • Moderation logOpen reports, warnings, auto-mod actions and full history. Resolve, dismiss or archive.

Announcements

  • ComposerTitle, body, image and a link button, with a live Discord-style preview before posting.
  • Role targetingPing a role, or post quietly.
  • From Discord too/announce shows you a private preview, then posts.

Onboarding

  • Auto roleNew members get your member role, and Bastion fixes any it missed.
  • Welcome messagesCustom message with the member's name, your server name and your rules channel.
  • Game update roles/setup-updates posts a panel where members choose which update pings they get.

Server management

  • Channels & categoriesCreate, rename, move and delete, with guards on destructive actions.
  • Permission presetsPublic, members-only, staff-only, read-only or hidden in one click.
  • Staff accessChoose which roles can use which Bastion tools.

Safety & records

  • Health checksPermissions, intents, roles and channels, each with a recommended fix.
  • Audit logEvery staff and bot action, filterable and exportable.
  • Structure snapshotsAutomatic copies of your channels, roles and permissions, compared against the live server.
  • Settings backupsBack up and restore Bastion's own settings.

Account & updates

  • One simple licenseOne server and one PC per subscription. If a payment fails you get 7 days, then read-only mode. Your data is never deleted.
  • Automatic updatesDownloaded and checked before they install, and your settings stay put.
  • Status check/status shows how the bot is doing, right from Discord.
