Questions

Is Bastion safe to install on my PC?

Bastion is a regular Windows app that runs under your own account. It connects to Discord to run your bot, and to BVNGER only to check your license and download updates. Your bot token is encrypted for your Windows user, and your server settings, records and backups stay on your computer. We don't collect your server's messages or members, and we never sell data. You can stop it anytime and uninstall it like any other app. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what it stores and where.

Does my computer need to stay on?

On Standard, yes, for features that respond to members, such as tickets, reports, welcome messages and the word filter. Posting announcements, moderating and managing channels only need Bastion running while you use them. The Hosted plan will remove this requirement.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime from the billing link in your receipt email. Bastion keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.

Is full server restore included?

Snapshots and read-only comparison are included now. Full server structure restore is still in development and will arrive as an update at no extra cost.

Is my Discord bot token sent to you?

No. On Standard, your bot token stays on your computer. See the Privacy Policy for details.
